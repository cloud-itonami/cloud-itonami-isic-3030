Aircraft and Aerospace Manufacturing Enablement

ISIC 3030 · maturity implemented

Open Business Blueprint for ISIC Rev.5 3030: aircraft and aerospace manufacturing enablement -- airframe assembly, composite layup and verification.

This repository publishes an aerospace-manufacturing actor -- assembly intake, requirements verification, NDT-defect screening, assembly- action dispatch and airworthiness-evidence issuance -- as an OSS business that any qualified manufacturer can fork, deploy, run, improve and sell.
